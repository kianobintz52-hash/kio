Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Can't find what you're looking for? Join our Discord.

What is fakecrime.bio? ▼
fakecrime.bio is a profile page platform where you can showcase your identity, links, badges, music tracks, and more — all from a single customizable page.
Is fakecrime.bio free to use? ▼
Yes! fakecrime.bio is completely free. A Premium tier is available for users who want access to exclusive features like animated backgrounds, custom domains, and priority support.
How do I verify my account? ▼
Account verification is done through Discord. Head to your account settings, connect your Discord, and follow the verification steps. See the Verification guide for details.
Can I have multiple accounts? ▼
Multi-accounting is not permitted on fakecrime.bio. Each person is allowed one account. Violations may result in a permanent ban. See our Multi Accounting policy for more information.
How do I add a custom badge? ▼
Custom badges can be managed at fakecrime.bio/extras/badges. Premium users can upload custom images or animated GIFs as badges.
How do I delete my account? ▼
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Visit the Account Deletion page for instructions on how to proceed.
How do I get support? ▼
You can get support by joining our Discord server and opening a support ticket. Premium users receive priority support.

Configuration

Set up fakecrime.bio as your personal image host — perfect for ShareX, Flameshot, or any uploader tool.

Getting your API key

1
Open Account Settings

Event Rules

All participants must follow these rules to ensure fair and enjoyable events for everyone.

General Rules

The following rules apply to all fakecrime.bio events unless otherwise specified in the individual event announcement:

⚠️
Violations may result in disqualification from the current event and potentially from future events.

1. Fair Play

Any form of cheating, exploiting, or manipulating event systems is strictly prohibited. This includes using bots, alts, or coordinating to game leaderboard rankings.

2. One Account Per Person

Each participant may only compete with one account. Using multiple accounts to gain an advantage will result in a permanent ban from all events.

3. Respectful Conduct

Harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior toward other participants will not be tolerated and will result in immediate disqualification.

4. Submission Guidelines

All submissions must be your own original work. Plagiarism or submitting AI-generated content as your own (in art events) is not permitted.

5. Staff Decisions are Final

The fakecrime.bio team reserves the right to modify, cancel, or extend events, and their rulings on disputes are final.

Account

Common account issues and how to resolve them quickly.

I can't access my account ▼
Try resetting your password via the login page. If you signed up with Discord, make sure your Discord account is still active and try re-linking it.
My profile page is not showing up ▼
Profile pages may be temporarily unavailable if your account is under review. Check your email for any notices from the team, or contact support on Discord.
I was banned — what do I do? ▼
If you believe you were banned in error, open a support ticket in our Discord server with your username and a brief explanation. Do not create new accounts while banned.
I want to change my username ▼
Username changes are available as a one-time purchase from the Shop. Free username changes are not available.

Troubleshooting

Discord

Having trouble connecting your Discord account? Here's how to fix the most common issues.

Discord linking fails or shows an error ▼
Make sure you're logged into the correct Discord account in your browser. Try logging out of Discord, logging back in, then re-attempting the link from your fakecrime.bio settings.
My Discord is linked but I didn't get the server role ▼
Roles can take up to 5 minutes to sync. If it's been longer, try unlinking and re-linking your Discord, then rejoin the server if needed.
I changed my Discord account ▼
Go to Settings → Connections, unlink your old Discord, and link the new one. Note that verification resets when you unlink.
💬
Still stuck? Open a ticket in our Discord server and a staff member will help you out.

Troubleshooting

Login

Can't log in to fakecrime.bio? Work through these steps to get back into your account.

1
Check your credentials
Make sure you're using the correct email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
2
Reset your password
Click Forgot Password on the login page and follow the email instructions. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive.
3
Try Discord login
If you originally signed up with Discord, use the Login with Discord button instead of email/password.
4
Clear cookies and cache
Old session data can sometimes cause login loops. Clear your browser's cookies for fakecrime.bio and try again.
5
Contact support
If none of the above works, open a support ticket in Discord with your username and email address.

Account

Account Deletion

Account deletion is permanent. All your data, profile, links, images, and badges will be removed and cannot be recovered.

⚠️
This action is irreversible. Before proceeding, consider exporting your data or simply deactivating your profile instead.

How to delete your account

1
Go to Account Settings
Log in and navigate to Settings → Account → Danger Zone.
2
Click "Delete Account"
You'll be asked to confirm by typing your username. This is to prevent accidental deletions.
3
Confirm Deletion
After confirmation, your account is scheduled for deletion within 24 hours. You will receive a final email confirmation.

Account

Multi Accounting

Each person is permitted one account on fakecrime.bio. Multi-accounting undermines the fairness of the platform and will not be tolerated.

🚫
Multi-accounting is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent ban of all associated accounts.

What counts as multi-accounting?

Multi-accounting includes but is not limited to:

  • → Creating a new account after being banned
  • → Using multiple accounts to participate in the same event
  • → Sharing accounts between different people
  • → Creating an account for a bot or automated profile

Consequences

ViolationAction
First offense (minor)Warning + secondary account deleted
Evading a banPermanent ban on all accounts
Event manipulationDisqualification + ban

Exceptions

If you have a legitimate reason to need account help (e.g. you lost access to your old account), contact support before creating a new account. Staff can assist with account recovery without requiring you to create a second account.
